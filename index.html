Cut the ropes correctly — save the tiles, keep the gorillas hanging.

Swipe across a rope to cut it.
How to play: swipe (or tap) a rope to cut it. A tile mascot that drops a short way lands safely on the grass — one that falls too far gets knocked out. If a gorilla reaches the ground, it's free and the level is lost. Order matters: a hanging friend can lower another one.
